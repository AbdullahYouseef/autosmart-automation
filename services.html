01 — Lighting

Lighting Control

Every fixture in your home grouped into scenes: "Morning," "Movie Night," "Away." Lights respond to time of day, occupancy sensors, or a single tap on your phone or wall panel — no more walking room to room at bedtime.

  • Dimmable & color-tunable circuitsSupported
  • Occupancy & daylight sensingIncluded
  • Outdoor & landscape lightingOptional
  • Voice assistant integrationAlexa / Google / Siri

Typical scope

10–25 fixtures per home, wired to smart switches or in-fixture modules, grouped into 4–8 scenes and mapped to a wall panel and mobile app.
