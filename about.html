Built by people who install what they sell

AutoSmart Automation started as a two-person electrical crew that kept getting asked the same question: "can you make this house smarter?" Twelve years later, that's the whole business.

Our Story

We got tired of gimmicky smart home gear.

Most "smart home" products are sold as gadgets — a bulb here, a plug there — that stop talking to each other the moment you add a second brand. We started AutoSmart Automation to do the opposite: design a system first, then choose equipment that actually fits it.

Every technician on our team is licensed and does hands-on installs — nobody on-site is subcontracted labor meeting your home for the first time on install day.

What We Believe

Four things we won't compromise on.

Reliability

A smart home that fails when you need it most is worse than no automation at all. We over-engineer for uptime, not for demo-day flash.

Plain Answers

If a feature isn't worth the cost for your home, we'll say so — even if it means a smaller invoice.

Own the Work

The technician who designs your system is on-site for the install, and reachable afterward. No call centers.

Open Standards

We favor equipment that plays well with others, so you're never locked into one manufacturer's ecosystem.
